## 🚨 RULES FOR THIS FILE — READ FIRST

> ### ⚠️ `SUGGESTED — REQUIRES OWNER CONFIRMATION`
>
> **Every peso figure in this document is an industry estimate, not an EYG price.** EYG
> has never published a price anywhere (`facebook-intel.md` §2.9). The à-la-carte sums
> below are **calculated from the ranges in `content/services.md` §9** — they are
> arithmetic on guesses, not prices.
>
> **A bundle is the most legally exposed thing on the site.** It is a *package price
> contract*. If the customer arrives expecting the bundle and the à-la-carte total is
> higher — or the bundle quietly excludes the thing they thought was in it — that is a
> deceptive-practice shape, not a discount (`legal-compliance-ph.md` §4.2). **Every bundle
> must therefore state, in the same block, exactly what is included, what is not, and what
> the same work would cost separately.**

### The six package rules

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **R1** | **A bundle price is always lower than the sum of its parts** | Otherwise it is not a bundle. Compute it and show the saving. `PackageDto.savingsPct` exists for this |
| **R2** | **Print the à-la-carte sum next to the bundle price** | Transparency is the whole differentiator (`competitors.md` §3). It also makes the saving self-evidencing |
| **R3** | **Never build a bundle around an unconfirmed service** | Undercoating and roadside are blocked. Bundling them would mean selling a promise. `services.md` §3.2, §7.1 |
| **R4** | **"What's not included" is on the same card, same weight** | AP7 — the industry hides exclusions until handover. **This is the trust signal** |
| **R5** | **No urgency, no countdown, no "limited slots"** | N2. The shop's one real promo expired 2025-12-09. **Fabricating scarcity in a 138-business market where the shop's own customers can check is fatal** |
| **R6** | **The saving is real arithmetic, never inflated** | A "₱1,500 saving" on a bundle that was never priced à-la-carte is a fabricated claim |

**Why it exists — the rationale**

1. **It is genuinely useful, not a discount hook.** A tread-depth card is a real tool the
   customer keeps and can verify at home against the 1.6 mm minimum. That is honest
   value, and nobody in Balanga is giving it away (`competitors.md` §3).
2. **It is timed to a real, local, annual event.** Bataan gets the southwest monsoon;
   flooded roads, standing water, and debris. **Nobody publishes a seasonal offer here
   because nobody publishes anything** — so a genuine seasonal service is a genuine first
   move.
3. **It costs EYG almost nothing in stock.** The nitrogen top-up is free while the car is
   already on the lift, and the inspection is labour EYG performs anyway during a PMS.
4. **It is a low-risk door-opener.** A customer who comes for a free-ish safety check and a

   come back. **The report is the product; the sale is the side effect.**
5. **It is the one package that can be honestly advertised without a price claim.** Even
   unconfirmed, "tyre inspection + wiper replacement + brake check" is defensible, because
   the inspection, the wiper fitting and the brake measurement are all **inside EYG's
   verified service list** (Tire Mounting and Repair · Wheel Balancing/Alignment family ·
   Brake Cleaning and Maintenance). **All three components are verified.** This package
   needs no new capability.

**🔴 Owner confirmation needed:** real price · do you fit customer-supplied wipers, or
sell your own? · is the nitrogen top-up genuinely free? · **is the 1.6 mm figure your
policy** — it is the widely-cited Philippine minimum, and printing it publicly is a
commitment to measuring honestly.

# The `/deals` and `/packages` copy rules

> `/deals` is on the locked page map (`AGENT-BRIEF.md` §3) for *"Seasonal promos, tyre
> clearance, bundles"*. The honest problem: **the only promo EYG has ever run is expired.**
> So `/deals` has one honest default — **it shows the bundles, not a fake sale.**

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| **D1** | **Show the bundles. Do not invent a countdown.** A `/deals` page with no active promo should present the **six packages as always-available**, plus a genuinely seasonal service when the season is real. **An empty `/deals` is fine. A fake `/deals` is not.** |
| **D2** | 🚫 **No "ends in 03:42:17". No "3 slots left". No "SALE — 50% OFF".** N2. **Shop capacity is modelled at 3 bays with a 12:00–13:00 break** (`src/config/site.ts`) — so any scarcity number is checkable, and checking it is exactly what a 308-follower audience will do |
| **D3** | 🔴 **Never reuse the expired opening promo.** It is dated 2025-12-09 and is in the
public record. `facebook-intel.md` §2.8. N21 |
| **D4** | **If a real promo runs, it needs: real dates, real quantities, real terms, and a
written "what's excluded".** A promo with exclusions hidden in small print is the bait-and-switch
pattern in its purest form. `legal-compliance-ph.md` §4.2 |
