# VOICE & TONE — EYG TIRE & AUTO CARE

Version 1.0 · October 2026

## 1. Who we sound like

A **competent mechanic who answers the phone**. Not a marketing department.

The audience is a person who is either (a) **stranded on EGSA Fourlanes with a
flat** at 6 pm with a truck behind them, or (b) **planning** a set of tyres for
a 40-minute-each-way commute. They are price-conscious, on a phone, in traffic,
and they have been oversold to by everyone else on that road.

### The promise in one line

> Straight answers, fair prices, and the bay is ready when you arrive.

### 1.1 Voice vs tone

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Voice** (fixed, never changes) | Direct. Practical. Motored. Honest. Filipino. |
| **Tone** (flexible, changes with the moment) | See the tone ladder in §4. |

## 2. The five voice principles

### 2.1 Direct

One idea per sentence. Say the thing. Then stop.

| ❌ | ✅ |
| --- | --- |
| "We're excited to help you with all your automotive tyre needs!" | "Tyres, alignment, brakes, undercoating. What does your car need?" |
| "Please do not hesitate to reach out to our team." | "Call us." |
| "We offer a comprehensive suite of vehicle maintenance solutions." | "We fix cars." |

- **Lead with the answer.** Not the context, not the preamble, not the brand

  name. If they asked about tyres, tyres come first.

- **Sentences under ~18 words** in anything above a heading.
- **No rhetorical questions** in body copy. ("Worried about your tyres?")
- **No exclamation marks** in transactional copy. One per Facebook post maximum,

  and never in a price, a disclaimer, or an error.

### 2.2 Practical

Say what happens, how long, and what it costs. In that order.

```text
   PMS A — up to 4 hours, from ₱1,200 (standard passenger car)
   We check alignment and tyre pressure before we start, and we tell you
   what we find. If it needs more, we call you first.
```

Not:

```text
   Our state-of-the-art preventive maintenance programme delivers
   uncompromising protection for your investment.
```

- **Every service has a time and a price range.** If we don't know, say

  "we'll confirm after inspection" and **mean it**.

- **Say what we will NOT do.** "We don't do engine rebuilds" is more

  trustworthy than silence.

### 2.3 Motored

The brand's register is motorsport: fast, competent, unglamorous, precise.

#### Words we use

> bay · slot · pit · checkered · straight · clean · set · press · torque ·
> align · balance · tread · kerb · roadside · breakdown · ayusin

##### Words we avoid

> synergistic · curated · holistic · premium · seamless · cutting-edge ·
> journey · unlock · elevate · solution (as a noun) · hassle-free ·
> one-stop-shop · state-of-the-art

###### The grammar of it

- ✅ **Imperative and first person.** "Book a bay." "We call you first."

  "Bring the car."

- ❌ **Passive corporate voice.** "Your appointment has been scheduled" →

  "Your bay is booked."

- ❌ **Nominalisation.** "We performed an alignment" → "We aligned it."

### 2.4 Honest

This is the constraint that overrides every marketing instinct.

#### We never claim anything the shop has not signed off on

| ❌ Never ship | ✅ Say instead |
| --- | --- |
| "Authorised Michelin dealer" (unless there is a written agreement) | "We fit Michelin, Bridgestone and Goodyear." (a factual statement about stock) |
| "4.9 ★ from 200+ customers" | Nothing, until it is real and sourced. |
| "Certified technicians" | "Our technicians." |
| "10 years of experience" | Nothing, until confirmed. |
| "Licensed and insured" | Nothing, until confirmed. |
| "Free pickup and delivery" | Nothing, until it's actually offered. |
| "Open 24/7" | The real hours, from `BUSINESS_HOURS`. |
| "Satisfaction guaranteed" | The actual warranty terms, with a number and a duration. |

##### Other honesty rules

- ❌ **Never invent customer names, quotes or reviews.** Ever. Not a composite,

  not a "typical customer", not a first-name-only quote.

- ❌ **Never use stock photography as EYG's work.** A photo of someone else's

  bay is a lie the customer will discover in person.

- ❌ **Never round a price up in the copy and down in the quote.**
- ✅ **Publish ranges, and say they are ranges.**
- ✅ **Say what we don't know.** "We won't know until it's on the lift" is

  respected; a wrong confident answer is not.

### 2.5 Filipino

Plain English that a Taglish-speaking Balanga customer reads instantly.
Respectful, not cute.

#### Use the vocabulary locals actually use

> PMS · PMS A/B · wheel alignment · tire rotation · vulcanizing ·
> undercoating · change oil · brake pad · shock absorbers · CVL / bushings ·
> car aircon · roadside · breakdown · ayusin / check · CVO (deferred down
> payment) · bay · pit

##### Language rules

- ✅ **English as the base.** Every customer, staff member and supplier reads

  it.

- ✅ **Filipino automotive nouns are not "borrowed words", they are the

  product names.** Use them.

- ✅ **Respectful "po" when a customer's own message uses it.** Mirror the

  register; don't add it to a broadcast post.

- ❌ **Never full-sentential Tagalog.** It excludes English-speaking staff and

  customers and reads as a costume.

- ❌ **Never use "kami po" theatrically in a headline.** It is a small shop

  where everyone knows the names.

- ❌ **Never use Filipino slang as humour.** A customer with a broken-down car

  is not being entertained.

- ❌ **Never translate an English brand name.** "PMS", not "preventive

  maintenance service" in a headline.

## 3. Vocabulary — the house list

| Use | Not |
| --- | --- |
| bay | garage, workshop, service center, auto shop |
| book / booking | reserve, schedule, appointment, slot booking |
| call | ring, dial, contact us, get in touch |
| slot | window, timeslot, availability |
| service | solution, offering, package (unless it *is* a bundle) |
| price range | starting at (unless it truly is), inquire for price, "affordable" |
| customer | client, patron, valued customer, "sir/madam" as a default |
| we | the team, EYG would like to assist |
| breakdown / roadside | emergency, roadside assistance *(only if actually offered)* |
| tyre | tire — **both are correct**; match the customer's usage. The registered name uses **"Tire"**. The brand ramp is consistent in American spelling, so "tire" throughout |
| technician | mechanic, our specialist, technician *(all fine — "our mechanic" is friendliest)* |
| receipt | invoice *(invoice is correct for a business customer)* |
| GCash | Gcash, GCash (correct capitalisation) |
| Maya | Maya (correct), "Maya Money" (also acceptable) |
| ₱ | PHP, Php, P |

### 3.1 Capitalisation & punctuation

- **Sentence case for headings.** "Wheel alignment, not a guess." Not "Wheel

  Alignment, Not A Guess."

- **All caps only** for `.eyg-eyebrow` labels, in the logo, and in Bay signage

  set as a display element.

- **Serial comma** in lists: "tyres, alignment, and brakes."
- **Em dash** for an aside: "Book a bay — or call, if you're stranded."
- **En dash** for ranges: "Mon–Sat", "8 am–5 pm", "₱1,200–₱1,600".
- **The peso sign is attached and has no space**: `₱1,200`. Always.
- **Numbers under 10 in body copy** as words, but **always** as digits in

  prices, times, phone numbers, quantities, sizes, and reference codes.

## 4. The tone ladder

Tone is a dial, not a switch. Pick by **what the customer is doing right now**.

| Lane | Situation | Tone | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — Emergency** | Stranded, on the road, in a hurry | **Fast, calm, one instruction at a time.** No brand voice at all. No emoji. No exclamation. | "Call us now: [number]. Stay in the car with the hazards on. We'll tell you whether to drive it in or leave it." |
| **B — Planned** | Booking, comparing, checking prices | **Plain, specific, unfussy.** Facts, then the action. | "PMS A runs up to 4 hours and starts at ₱1,200 for a standard passenger car. Bring it in between 8 am and 5 pm, Monday to Saturday. Book a bay below." |
| **C — Re-engagement** | Saw a promo, came back | **Energetic but brief.** There is a deadline and that is the whole message. | "4+4 on selected sizes. Ends 30 November. Claim it below." |

### Rules across all three lanes

- The phone number and the price are never more than **one scroll** from the top.
- No tone above Lane C. EYG is not a lifestyle brand.
- Lane A copy never contains a promo, a cross-sell, or a brand flourish.

## 5. Channel by channel

### 5.1 The phone (the top of the funnel)

The most important channel. 308 followers on a 308-person team — trust is
per-person.

- **Answer with your name and the shop's name**: "EYG Tire, this is [name]."
- **One question, then listen.** Do not run a script.
- **Confirm what you heard**: "So, PMS A on a [make model], tyres included.

  Around ₱1,200, up to four hours. Bay's open at 2."

- **Never** quote a price you would not honour.
- **If you don't know**, say so and say when you will call back.

### 5.2 Facebook — the shop's front door

- **Name people.** The people at EYG are the brand. "Rodel aligned your

  Rav4" beats "we completed a wheel alignment service".

- **Post photos of the real work.** Before/after pairs, shot per the

  photography standard.

- **Reply to every comment**, especially the pricing ones. Silence on a price

  question reads as "expensive".

- **Never** boost or boost-post a claim you can't evidence.
- **Emoji**: at most one per post, and never in a price, a disclaimer, an

  error, or a safety message. Zero is the default.

### 5.3 The website

- **Every page has one job.** One conversion. One primary action.
- **The phone number is always within one thumb-reach.** Persistent bottom bar

  on mobile. It is the highest `z-index` on the page.

- **Every form has pending, success and error states**, and none of them fails

  silently.

- **No modal, no cookie wall, no interstitial.** Ever. It blocks the one

  action that matters.

- **Price ranges on the page.** "Inquire for price" is a dead end.

### 5.4 SMS / WhatsApp confirmations

Transactional, terse, and useful:

```text
EYG Tire & Auto Care
Bay booked — Thu 16 Oct, 10:00
PMS A + 2 tyres (195/65 R15)
Ref: EYG-4F2A
EGSA Fourlanes, Tuyo, Balanga
Reply STOP to opt out.
```

- Reference codes are **monospace, uppercase, and short** so they can be read

  aloud over the phone without ambiguity.

- Never send a promo SMS to someone who has not opted in.

### 5.5 The job card / receipt

Formal, complete, plain:

```text
  EYG Tire & Auto Care
  EGSA Fourlanes, Tuyo · Balanga City, Bataan 2100
  [phone]                                    EYG-4F2A
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  16 Oct 2026 · 10:00                Bay 2            Ref EYG-4F2A
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  SERVICE                        QTY          AMOUNT
  PMS A                            1          1,200.00
  Tyre 195/65 R15                   2          9,800.00
  Wheel alignment                   1            600.00
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                                       TOTAL   11,600.00
  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Cash / GCash / Maya / Card / Credit Instalment
  Prices confirmed before work started.
  [printed disclaimer, 7pt minimum]
```

- **Always** the reference, the date, the bay number, and the printout of what

  was agreed before work started.

- **Nothing smaller than 7 pt.**

### 5.6 Signage

- **Wordmark first.** Then, at most, four words: `TYRES · ALIGNMENT · BRAKES ·

  UNDERCOATING`.

- **The phone number on any roadside sign must be the largest type on it** —

  larger than the logo. That is the conversion.

- **No adjectives.** No "QUALITY SERVICE". No "BEST PRICES".

## 6. Claims policy — hard rules

### 6.1 The `TODO-VERIFY` wall

Every value in `src/config/site.ts` flagged `TODO-VERIFY` is **unconfirmed**:
the phone numbers, the email addresses, the coordinates, the Messenger handle,
the warranty days, the rating, the years in business, the bay count, the
technician count, the partner brands, and the social handles.

> **None of these may appear on any published or printed artefact until the
> owner confirms them.** Not on a poster. Not on a Facebook post. Not on a
> business card.

This is not caution for its own sake: publishing a wrong phone number on a
sign beside a highway costs the shop a day of walk-ins, and publishing an
unearned trust claim costs it the trust that a 308-follower page runs on.

### 6.2 Claim checklist before publishing

- [ ] Is every number here sourced from `site.ts`, and no longer `TODO-VERIFY`?
- [ ] Is every brand name here a brand the shop **actually stocks or is authorised to sell**?

- [ ] Have I written the price **range**, and labelled it a range?
- [ ] Is there a real end date on the promo?
- [ ] Are the faces released and the plates blurred?
- [ ] Is this our photo, not stock?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if the customer compared this claim to what they actually got?

## 7. Reading list

| Term | EYG rule |
| --- | --- |
| "We" / "our" | Preferred. First person, active. |
| The customer | "you", "your car". Never "the client". |
| "The shop" | Once, at most, in about copy. Not in copy to a customer. |
| Emojis | Zero by default. One maximum per social post. |
| Exclamation marks | One maximum per social post. Never transactional. |
| Contractions | Yes, freely — "you're", "we'll", "it's". They sound like a person. |
| Taglish particles | Not in broadcast copy. Fine in a reply to a customer who used them. |
| Jargon | Only automotive jargon the customer already uses. Explain the rest. |
| Slang | Never as humour. Never at a distressed customer. |
| Sympathy | Once, briefly, then solve it. "Sorry you're stuck — call us now." |

## 8. Do / Don't

### ✅ DO

- ✅ Answer first, then explain.
- ✅ Say the price, the time, and what happens next.
- ✅ Say "we don't know yet, we'll call you by X".
- ✅ Say what we don't do.
- ✅ Use the vocabulary locals use: bay, PMS, alignment, ayusin, CVO.
- ✅ Publish ranges and label them ranges.
- ✅ Use people's names, and get permission to use a face.
- ✅ Confirm everything on the job card before the work starts.

### ❌ DON'T

- ❌ Invent a phone number, a price, a rating, a warranty, a year count, or a

  partnership.

- ❌ Write "authorised dealer" / "certified" / "licensed" for anything not

  documented.

- ❌ Fake a review or a customer name.
- ❌ Use stock photos as EYG's work.
- ❌ Write "Inquire for price" anywhere.
- ❌ Use corporate filler: "solutions", "seamless", "premium", "we're

  passionate".

- ❌ Overwrite. Every sentence that does not inform or convert is deleted.
- ❌ Use a full-stop corporate tone with a stranded customer.
- ❌ Publish anything still flagged `TODO-VERIFY`.
