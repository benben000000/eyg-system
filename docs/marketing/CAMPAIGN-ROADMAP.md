# CAMPAIGN ROADMAP — EYG Tire & Auto Care

> **Owner:** marketing agent · **Horizon:** 12 months, 4 quarters
> **Premise:** this is a small shop with roughly three people who also do the
> actual work. Every campaign below is sized so it can survive contact with a
> real week.

---

## 0. The honest version

Before the quarters, three things a strategy document normally hides.

### 1. What this shop can actually sustain

| Resource | Realistic capacity |
| --- | --- |
| Owner / manager time on marketing | **4–6 hours a week.** Not more. Marketing that needs 15 hours a week will happen for three weeks and then never again. |
| Facebook posts | **5–6 a week**, most of them a phone photo taken on a Saturday |
| Google Business Profile posts | **1 a week** |
| Review requests | **Every single job** (this is free and it is the highest-ROI activity) |
| Email sends | **1–2 a month** |
| SMS marketing sends | **1 per 21 days maximum** |
| Paid ads | **₱0/day until Q3**, then ₱20/day |
| Time to reply to a Messenger DM | **Under 5 minutes, during shop hours.** This is not marketing. It is the business. |

**A quarter that assumes 8 hours a week of marketing is a quarter that will fail.** Every campaign below is costed in hours.

### 2. What actually drives growth here

Ranked by expected return on the owner's time, not by how it looks on a plan:

| Rank | Activity | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Asking every customer for a review, at handover** | Free. Every time. Compounds forever. |
| **2** | **Answering Messenger in under 5 minutes** | The top of funnel. Free. No competitor beats a fast reply. |
| **3** | **Google Business Profile completeness + photos** | Free. The Local Pack. Compounds forever. |
| **4** | **Word of mouth (referral, with a ₱300 thank-you)** | The main engine. Just make it sayable. |
| **5** | **One strong campaign at a time** | Concentrated, not constant. |
| **6** | **Educational Facebook content** | Slow, then very effective. Earns saves and shares. |
| **7** | Email newsletter | Real, but slow. |
| **8** | Paid ads | Last. Only with spare capacity. |

**The uncomfortable implication:** paid ads and email are ranked below Facebook
and reviews. That is correct for this shop. Most marketing plans for small local
businesses get this backwards and then blame the algorithm.

### 3. The capacity ceiling

> **If the bay is 70%+ booked on a normal day, do not run a campaign.**
> Discounts do not create capacity. They create a queue, a grumpy mechanic and
> a customer who waited.

**The test:** "Are we turning people away on a Tuesday?" If yes — no campaign.
Fix the bay first, then sell.

## Q1 — GET FOUND

**Duration:** 3 months · **Owner time:** ~5 hrs/week · **Cash:** ~₱2,000–3,000 (photography, printed cards)

**The goal is not sales. The goal is to exist in Google's index, to have a page
people can trust, and to answer fast.**

### The problem

A shop with 308 followers and no Google Business Profile presence is invisible
to `tire shop near me`. In 2026, that query is worth more than any Facebook
campaign.

### Launches, in order

| # | Launch | Owner hrs | Owner commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Confirm the facts** — phone, hours, coordinates, founding year, which services are real | 1 | Confirm every `TODO-VERIFY` in `site.ts` |
| 2 | **Google Business Profile, complete** — name, NAP, hours, categories, 20 services, 10 products, attributes | 3 | None |
| 3 | **12 photos, real, taken on one Saturday morning** | 3 | Be in the photos. Get consent from the team. |
| 4 | **20 seeded Q&As on GBP** | 2 | Answer Q19 honestly |
| 5 | **Fast-reply protocol** — 5-minute rule, quick replies installed, one person owns the page | 1 | Someone must actually answer. |
| 6 | **Review card at handover** — laminated card + Taglish script + the app on the phone at the counter | 1 | Ask **every** customer. Always. |
| 7 | **Rainy Season Safety Bundle** campaign (Jun–Nov window) | 1 | Confirm the price |
| 8 | **First Visit PMS 15%** campaign | 1 | Confirm the price |
| 9 | **Facebook: launch the content bank at 6 posts/week** | 2/week sustained | Shoot on Saturday |
| 10 | **Tier 1–3 citations** — 20 sites, full NAP | 3 | None — delegate this |
| 11 | **GBP weekly posts (1/week)** | 0.5/week | None |

### Weekly rhythm

```
Mon  30 min  Review GBP insights + reply to reviews
Mon  30 min  Post (scheduled) + GBP post
Wed  30 min  Customer-moment capture — photograph 2 jobs
Thu  30 min  Reply to all DMs, chase quotes
Sat  30 min  Shoot Saturday's bank post + 3 photos for GBP
Sun  15 min  Week review: what did we do, what did we skip?
```

### Success criteria (all four, or Q1 has not worked)

- [ ] Google Business Profile is 100% complete, zero pending items
- [ ] **20+ Google reviews** (≈2/week, every week, no exceptions)
- [ ] Average rating ≥ 4.5
- [ ] Median Messenger reply time **under 5 minutes** during shop hours
- [ ] Website has real contact details and a real phone number in it
- [ ] 30+ Facebook posts published
- [ ] 20 citations live with identical NAP
- [ ] **20+ people asked the owner for a real review** (the direct-to-past-customers
      list is the single highest-ROI task in the quarter)

### What NOT to launch in Q1

❌ Paid ads · ❌ Email newsletter · ❌ A second campaign in one category · ❌ A
rebrand · ❌ A TikTok · ❌ Loyalty card (no data yet to justify it)

### Known risks in Q1

| Risk | Signal | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Low review volume | < 1/week by week 3 | Go back to the past-customer list. This is the first thing to fix. |
| Photo shoot never happens | 4 weeks, no photos | Do it Saturday morning with a phone. Not a professional shoot. Just do it. |
| Slow Messenger replies | "busy" excuse appears twice | The reply queue belongs to one person. Not whoever is nearest. |
| Campaign price wrong | Counter disputes a promo price | Remove the price from copy entirely. Offer the bundle without the number. |

## Q2 — EARN TRUST

**Duration:** 3 months · **Owner time:** ~5 hrs/week · **Cash:** ~₱3,000–5,000

**The goal: make price transparency the brand. Get 60+ reviews. Prove one
campaign works.**

### Launches, in order

| # | Launch | Owner hrs | Owner commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Review engine at full speed** — 2+/week, every job, reply to all | 0.5/week | Non-negotiable |
| 2 | **"We confirm before we start"** — publish the promise on the site, the wall, and the invoice | 2 | **This is an operating rule, not a slogan** |
| 3 | **The inspection sheet** — a printed one-page checklist, given to every customer | 2 | Build the template, then use it |
| 4 | **Facebook: trust fortnight every month** — the "a job we don't do", "we show you the old part", "the check sheet" posts | 1/week | Approve real photos |
| 5 | **Tyre Clearance campaign** (Oct–Dec window) | 1 | Confirm price and stock |
| 6 | **Referral campaign** — "Bring a Neighbour", ₱300 both sides | 1 | Keep it sayable |
| 7 | **GBP service descriptions** — add confirmed prices only to services the owner has verified | 2 | Verify each price |
| 8 | **Community content block** — 1 post/week on Bataan rain, EGSA traffic, holiday travel | 1/week | Just post |
| 9 | **First email newsletter** — to the Q1 email list, once | 2 | Write it once, properly |
| 10 | **Quarterly: review the winning bank posts** — cut what flopped, double what worked | 1 | Honest assessment |

### The "we do not do" post

Not a marketing asset — an **operational commitment**. Posting that you refuse
certain work means you must actually refuse it. If the shop starts taking those
jobs to hit a number, the post becomes a lie and the whole trust position
collapses.

### Success criteria

- [ ] **60+ Google reviews**, average ≥ 4.5
- [ ] Discovery search terms show "tire shop balanga" or similar in the top 10
- [ ] At least 1 campaign measured: leads, bookings, revenue, margin
- [ ] Website `GA4` or manual log shows the top 3 sources of calls
- [ ] "Price before we start" is printed on the invoice
- [ ] 60+ Facebook posts published
- [ ] 1 email newsletter sent, with recorded consent on every subscriber
- [ ] **Capacities known**: average bay occupancy on a Tuesday

### What NOT to launch in Q2

❌ Paid ads (still not — no data on margin yet) · ❌ A loyalty app · ❌
Rebuilding the website · ❌ More than one campaign per category

### Known risks in Q2

| Risk | Signal | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reviews stall at ~35 | Under 2/week for a fortnight | The ask has stopped happening. Put the card back on the counter and the script back in the mouth. |
| "We confirm before we start" breaks under pressure | A mechanic quotes verbally and moves on | The owner must enforce it. This is the one promise that cannot be broken once published. |
| Customers refuse to leave reviews | Pushback at handover | Ask anyway, every time. Never offer anything. Never ask for 5 stars. |
| Referral offer not used | Zero claims in 2 months | The mechanic is not mentioning it. Put it on the wall, and on the invoice. |

## Q3 — CONVERT

**Duration:** 3 months · **Owner time:** ~6 hrs/week · **Cash:** ~₱10,000–15,000 (ads, if launched)

**The goal: the website books bays. The profile ranks. The reviews are an asset.**

### Launches, in order

| # | Launch | Owner hrs | Owner commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Website conversion work** — check every page loads on a ₱3,000 Android on 3G; every form has a real confirm state; every page has one obvious next step | 3 | Test on a real old phone. |
| 2 | **Book tracking** — where did this booking come from? One field, always asked | 0.5/week | Ask every single time |
| 3 | **Undersold: roadside, as a channel** — the shop's highest-margin service, currently barely marketed | 2 | Be reachable. Set the call-out area and price. |
| 4 | **Meta ads, ₱20/day test** — 3 ad sets per `META-ADS.md` | 2 setup + 0.5/week | None — but only if Q3's precondition is met. |
| 5 | **Mechanical birthday / loyalty mechanic** | 2 | Requires a real consenting family each month |
| 6 | **Google: check the pin weekly; add photo each week** | 0.5/week | Keep it up |
| 7 | **Duplicate listing sweep** | 1 | None |
| 8 | **Email newsletter, monthly** | 1/month | Write it |
| 9 | **Seasonal re-arm** — rainy season or Undas, whichever the calendar says | 1 | Confirm prices |
| 10 | **Mid-Year Check-Up, book-ahead** (Jan/Feb post for May–Jul window) | 1 | Confirm price |

### The Meta ads precondition

**Do not launch until all three are true:**
1. GBP is complete with 20+ reviews.
2. Bay is 60%+ occupied on a normal day.
3. There are real bay photos.

If any is false, **reallocate the ₱600/month to the past-customer review
list.** It will outperform ads at this stage. Every peso.

### Success criteria

- [ ] **100+ Google reviews**, average ≥ 4.5, no rating dip
- [ ] Top 3 in the Local Pack for "tire shop Balanga" (check monthly, incognito)
- [ ] **≥ 15 bookings a month from the website** (not a number pulled from thin
      air — a real number, from the booking log)
- [ ] Website conversion rate measurable
- [ ] Cost per booked job from ads known, and **profitable or paused**
- [ ] Call source tracked: Facebook / Google / walk-in / referral
- [ ] Call-back list: every quote that did not convert, followed up

### What NOT to launch in Q3

❌ A second paid campaign · ❌ A franchise or reseller offer · ❌ A second
location · ❌ Influencer marketing · ❌ A "free" loyalty tier that costs real
money

### Known risks in Q3

| Risk | Signal | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ads burn ₱600, zero bookings | 14-day decision tree ends ❌ | Pause. Reallocate to reviews. Do not "give it another month". |
| Website gets bookings, phone does not | Either one | Test both. The phone is still the top of funnel — do not cut it. |
| A bad 1-star review tanks the average | Score drops below 4.4 | Reply well, do not delete, do not panic-post. It recovers. |
| Capacity exceeded | Customers turned away | **Stop all campaigns.** Sell the capacity problem back to the marketing — a full bay is not a marketing failure, it is a hiring decision. |

## Q4 — SCALE CAREFULLY

**Duration:** 3 months · **Owner time:** ~6 hrs/week · **Cash:** ~₱15,000–25,000 (ads scaled, or not)

**The goal: profit per job known. Referral engine running. Ads proven or
killed. Decide whether to grow or to consolidate.**

### Launches, in order

| # | Launch | Owner hrs | Owner commitment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Know the margin.** Every service: labour time × bay rate + actual part cost. Know which jobs make money and which are a favour. | 4 | **Must be done before ads scale.** |
| 2 | **Scale what worked.** If ads proved out, ₱50–100/day into the one winning ad set. If not, stay at ₱20 or go to ₱0. | 1 setup | Decide, do not drift |
| 3 | **Make the referral automatic** — mention it on every invoice, on the wall, at handover | 1 | Say it every time |
| 4 | **Year-end campaign** — Christmas, the busiest retail period | 1 | Confirm price |
| 5 | **Holiday period: pre-book Undas slots** | 1 | Staff the calendar |
| 6 | **Full-year review** — the 12-month audit of every campaign, post, channel and hour spent | 3 | Be honest |
| 7 | **Plan next year** — next fiscal year's campaigns, in the same order | 2 | None |
| 8 | **Decide: grow or consolidate?** | — | The owner's call alone. This document should not make it. |

### The Year 1 review — the questions that matter

1. What did we actually spend, in hours, on marketing? Was it 4–6 a week?
2. Which single campaign produced the most booked jobs? Was it margin-positive?
3. Where did the highest-margin jobs come from — ads, reviews, or referral?
4. Did "we confirm before we start" survive contact with the busiest month?
5. How many reviews, and did the average hold above 4.5?
6. What did we post that flopped? Cut it permanently.
7. What did we **not** do because there was no capacity? Was that correct?
8. Would we rather have more of this business, or a better version of it?

### Success criteria

- [ ] **Every service has a known margin.** No exceptions.
- [ ] 150+ Google reviews
- [ ] **Referral claims tracked and growing** (≥ 15/quarter)
- [ ] Ads at a known, stable cost per booked job, or paused
- [ ] Full-year content review done, bank pruned
- [ ] **A written Year 2 plan from the owner**, based on real numbers

### What NOT to launch in Q4

❌ A second location · ❌ A franchise · ❌ "Sell the business" thinking · ❌
Over-hiring to serve ads · ❌ More than one campaign per category

### Known risks in Q4

| Risk | Signal | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ads scale, margin disappears | Revenue up, profit flat | Stop. Volume without margin is a hobby. |
| Owner time creeps past 10 hrs/week | Calendar slips | Cut a channel. Facebook organic is the first to go, not reviews. |
| The brand drifts back to "cheap shop" | Discount depth creeping | Re-read `PROMO-PLAYBOOK.md` §4. Enforce the ceiling. |
| A competitor opens nearby | Panic | Do not discount into it. Publish reviews, publish photos, reply fast. The shop with visible work wins. |

## What "success" looks like at the end of Year 1

⚠️ **These are targets for discussion with the owner, not forecasts. Real
numbers depend on the bay, the staff, and the market.**

| Metric | Q1 | Q4 | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google reviews | 20 | **150+** | The asset. Compounds forever. |
| Average rating | 4.5+ | **4.5+** | Trust. |
| Discovery searches (GBP) | Baseline | **3–5×** | Demand. |
| Website bookings/month | 0 | **15–30** | Conversion. |
| Messenger reply time | Unknown | **< 5 min** | The top of funnel. |
| Facebook posts published | 30 | **250+** | Consistency. |
| Referral claims | 0 | **15/quarter** | Word of mouth, made measurable. |
| Campaigns run | 2 | **8–12** | A disciplined cadence, not a stream. |
| Known margin per service | No | **Yes** | The thing that makes the rest sustainable. |

**The honest truth:** the biggest single number in that table is **reviews**. A
tyre shop in Balanga with 150 honest Google reviews and a visible bay will
out-compete almost any shop in the area, regardless of what else is running.
