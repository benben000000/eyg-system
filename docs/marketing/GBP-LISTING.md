## 6. Review-request copy

⚠️ **The short review URL is not created yet.**
Go to GBP → Reviews → Get more reviews → copy the short link. It is roughly 3×
more effective than a general `g.page` search link.

### Script — spoken at handover, invoice in hand

```
Salamat sa pagbisita. Kung maganda ang experience mo, isang maliit na review
lang ang tulong para makita kami ng mga kapitbahay namin. Kung may kulang,
sabihin mo lang dito — ayos lang kami. Walang pressure.
```

*("Thank you for visiting. If your experience was good, one small review really
helps so your neighbours can find us. If something was lacking, just say it
here — that's fine. No pressure.")*

### Card — front

```
Kumusta? Kung Okay sa amin, tulong lang kami sa isang maliit na review sa
Google. Kung hindi Okay, sabihin mo lang — ayos lang namin. Salamat!
```

### Card — back

```
Scan or search for our shop on Google Maps, and tap "Write a review".

No filter, no script — just whatever you actually experienced.
```

Thank you.

### SMS — 2 hours after completion

Verified at 129 characters (≤160):

```
EYG Tire: thanks for coming in today. A Google review helps us more than
anything: {{url}} Reply STOP to opt out.
```

Template key: `copy.ts` → `SMS_TEMPLATES.REVIEW_REQUEST`. Sent once per
completed booking. **Never** to a customer with an unresolved complaint.

### Public Facebook post for reviews

```
Salamat sa mga napunta sa amin.

Kung may minute kayo, isang Google review ang malaking tulong sa amin. Hindi
man lang kami malaki, pero marami sa Balanga ang nakakakita sa reviews.

Kung maganda ang inyo — sulat lang. Kung may kulang — sulat din. Kasi ang
totoo lang namin ang kailangan, at kung mali kami, ayusin namin.

Walang pressure, walang filter. Salamat.
```

### The four rules

1. Ask **everyone**. Selective review requests violate Google policy.
2. Offer **nothing** in exchange. No discount, no free wipe, no priority booking.
3. Ask for "an **honest** review", never "a 5-star review".
4. Reply to every review within 48 hours.

## 8. GBP posts — weekly cadence

**Minimum one per week.** Four-post rotation:

| Week | Type | Topic | Written where |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Update | The tyre-pressure tip — check the door jamb, not the tyre | `social.ts` `dayOffset: 3` |
| 2 | Offer | The live campaign | `promotions.ts` |
| 3 | Update | The four warning lights — which ones mean stop | `social.ts` `dayOffset: 27` |
| 4 | Offer | The live campaign, or the seasonal bundle | `promotions.ts` |

### Offer post template

```
{{Title}} — until {{real end date}}

{{One line: what is included.}}

{{The value, in words. Final price confirmed after inspection.}}
{{URL}}

Valid until {{end date}}. Cannot be combined with other offers.
```

### Rules

- The GBP post must match the website **exactly**. Same price, same end date,
  same terms. A mismatch between GBP and the site is a trust failure discovered
  at the counter.
- **Never** run two GBP offer posts in the same service category at once
  (`PROMO-PLAYBOOK.md` §7).
- **No countdown timer.**
- **No "limited time"** unless the date is real.
- Offers with an expiry need re-posting for the next campaign; a stale offer
  post is worse than none.
