# MESSENGER & WHATSAPP MACROS — EYG Tire & Auto Care

> **Owner:** marketing agent · **For:** whoever answers the page at 8 AM and
> whoever answers at 8 PM.
> **Rule for this file:** if a 20-year-old can read it, understand it, and send
> it without thinking, it is finished. If it needs nuance, it belongs in a call,
> not a chat.

---

## 0. How to use this file

### Setup (once, 15 minutes)

1. **Facebook Page → Settings → Inbox → Saved Replies.** Add each macro from
   this file. Name them `EYG-01` through `EYG-20` so they are findable in a
   hurry.
2. **WhatsApp Business (free app) → Business Tools → Quick Replies.** Same keys.
3. Print §6 (the red-flag protocol) and §7 (the never-promise list) and tape
   them next to the screen. Those two are the ones that matter at 2 AM.

### The four things every reply must do

| # | Do this | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Reply fast.** The first reply can be 30 seconds. It does not need to be the answer. | Speed is the entire competitive advantage a small shop has. Speed converts; accuracy alone does not. |
| 2 | **Answer the question that was asked.** Not the one you wish they had asked. | Vague answers feel like a dodge. |
| 3 | **Never promise a price.** Ever. | See §7. |
| 4 | **End with one next step.** Not three options. One. | Two CTAs means zero CTAs. |

### The tone

- Warm, plain, competent, neighbourly. Short sentences.
- Taglish is welcome. If a customer writes in Tagalog, reply in Tagalog.
- No exclamation-mark stacking. One is plenty.
- Never argue. Never defend. Never blame a previous technician (§7.5).
- If you are not sure, say so, and say who will be sure.

### Placeholder tokens

Replace before sending:

| Token | Example |
| --- | --- |
| `{{NAME}}` | Maria |
| `{{PLATE}}` | ABC 123 |
| `{{VEHICLE}}` | 2019 Fortuner |
| `{{BAY}}` | Bay 2, free around 10 AM |

## 2. Price questions

### EYG-04 — "Magkano?"

```
Magkano depends sa make, model at year ng sasakyan, at minsan sa kondisyon "pag nakita namin.

I-send mo lang ang make, model, year — bahala na kami magbigay ng range. Pag dating mo sa shop, titingnan namin muna, tapos sabihin namin sa exact price bago mag-start. Walang surprise sa resibo.
```

### EYG-05 — Follow-up if they only sent "magkano?" with no vehicle

```
Kailangan kaming ng make, model at year ng sasakyan para magbigay tayo ng walang maling presyo.

Format: Toyota Fortuner 2019, or Kia Sportage 2021 — ganon lang.
```

### EYG-06 — They want a firm number over chat

```
Hindi po namin masisagot ng exact number dito kasi hindi pa namin nakikita ang sasakyan. Malaki chance magkaiba.

Pero ganito po — magsend ka ng **PERSONAL na presyo** pag dating mo sa shop. Bago kami magbukas ng kahit ano, sasabihin namin sa exact. Kung sobra, hindi mo kailangang magbayad.
```

> This is the single most important macro in the file. See §7.1.

## 3. Time, logistics and stock

### EYG-07 — "Gaano katagal?"

```
Depende sa job at sa vehicle.

Para sa PMS, usually 1–2 hours. Brake pads, mga 2–3. Tire change, less than an hour kung naka-stock na.

Tatanungin muna namin ang sasakyan para bigyan ka ng totoong estimate, hindi yung generic na "mga 2 hours".
```

### EYG-08 — "Meron kayo ba ng [size]?"

```
Tatanungin muna namin yung rack.

I-send mo: **exact na size** (nakikita sa sidewall, like 205/55 R16) **o** make, model, year ng sasakyan.

Hindi namin sasabihing meron kaming stock hangga hindi namin sure — ayaw namin mag-promise ng wala.
```

### EYG-09 — "Puwede ba lang ako pumunta?"

Oo, puwede. Walk-in kami basta may bakante na bay.

Pero mas mabuting i-book mo muna para sure yung bay. Pwede rin tumawag o mag-message dito para i-reserve namin para sa iyo.
```

### EYG-10 — "Paano makakapunta?"

```
Nasa **EGSA Fourlanes, Tuyo, Balanga City** kami. Madaling makita mula sa main road.

I-book mo yung **Get directions** sa website namin (eygtireautocare.ph/contact) — may maps at Waze link.

Kung papunta ka na, magsend ka ng plate number at location mo para alam namin papunta ka na.
```

### EYG-11 — "Bukas ba kayo?" / hours

```
Oo, bukas kami **Lunes–Sabado, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM**. Linggo, sarado.

Laging updated yung hours sa website at sa page namin. Kung magkakaiba minsan (holiday, etc.), ipapost namin dito.

### EYG-12 — "Magkano kaya kayo?"/ closing time

```
Nagtatapos kami ng **5:00 PM** Lunes–Sabado.

Kung after ng 5 na, kaya naming tawagan para sa **roadside** — basta safe ka at nakadikit sa gilid. Sabihin mo lang kung saan ka.

## 5. Safety and diagnosis

### EYG-15 — "Do you do motorcycles?"

```
Depende sa size at type ng bisikleta.

I-send mo lang yung **make at model**, sasabihin namin agad kung kaya namin o hindi. Kung hindi, tuturo kami sa iyo kung saan ka pupunta.
```

> ⚠️ **This macro is safe because it refuses to promise. Do not change it to
> "yes" without owner confirmation.**

### EYG-16 — "Safe pa ba mag-drive ng ganito ang tire ko?"

```
**Huwag mag-drive ng malayo.**

Kung **squeal** lang pag brake — madalas yan ay sinasabi lang na nag-iipon na yung pads. Pero kung **grinding** na — yung pad wala na, metal na sa metal na. Ayon doon, bumibilis yung pagkasira.

Kung kaya mo, **punta ka sa pinakamalapit na shop agad**, dahan-dahan, walang biglang brake. Kung tuluy-tuloy na papunta ka, **tawagan mo kami** — sasabihin namin kung safe pa mag-drive or dapat doon ka na huminto.
```

### EYG-17 — "Kekerwinin mo ba yung brakes ko?"

```
Oo, **check** lang — libre yung pag-check ng brake pads. Tatanggalin namin yung wheel, tatasukin yung pad, tsaka sasabihin namin kung gaano katanda.

Kung buo pa, sasabihin namin na buo pa. Hindi namin sasabihing kailangan palitan kung hindi naman.
```

### EYG-18 — "Nag-aanko na yung steering ko / umaanib"

```
Maraming dahilan — **alignment** ang pinaka-common, minsan **CVL o bushings**, minsan **brakes**.

Ipakita mo sa amin. Titingnan namin muna yung brakes kasi mas mahal yun kapag hindi namin na-check. Pagkatapos titingnan namin alignment, CVL at bushings.

Kung ayaw mong magpakita, atleast **humingi ng bayon** para makita mong safe ka mag-drive hanggang dito.
```

### EYG-19 — "Nag-turn signal na ako / nag-aalam na ako"

```
Kung **red ang oil pressure**, **temperature**, **brake system**, o **battery** — **ihinto mo agad yung sasakyan**.

Kung **engine check** lang o **tire pressure** — kaya pa ring mag-drive ng maingat, pero dapat i-check agad.

Kung unsure ka kung anong ilaw, **larawanin mo at i-send dito**. Sasabihin namin kung puwede pa bang mag-drive o dapat huminto ka na.
```

## 6. 🔴 RED-FLAG PROTOCOL

> **Print this. Tape it next to the screen. Read it at 2 AM.**

### The rule

If a customer describes a vehicle that could kill someone on the road —
**including themselves — you stop selling and start protecting.**

### 🔴 RED — do not let them drive. Tell them to stop

| Signal | What to say |
| --- | --- |
| Tire completely flat, sidewall visible or bulging | Do not drive |
| Tire showing the canvas / steel belts | Do not drive |
| Brake pedal goes to the floor | Do not drive |
| Fluid under the car, near the wheels | Do not drive |
| Steam or a burning smell | Shut the engine off, get out, move away |
| Oil pressure light on | Stop immediately |
| Temperature light on | Stop, shut off, wait for it to cool |
| Sparking from under the bonnet | Shut off, step back, call us |
| Smell of fuel inside the cabin | Stop, get out |
| Headlights not working, at night | Do not drive at night |
| Anything dripping that they cannot identify | Do not drive |

### The script

```
{{NAME}}, **huwag mo na i-drive yan.** Hindi yan safe.

Ito ang gagawin mo:
1. **Hazard lights** on.
2. **Engine off.** Key out.
3. **Seatbelt** on.
4. Nasa safe area ka? **Lapit sa shoulder, hindi sa fast lane.**
5. Naka-**park** ba? Tanggalin mo key, i-lock mo.
6. Tawagan mo ako dito.

Naiintindihan ko kasi abala ka. Pero mas mabuting tumigil ng 10 minuto kaysa may masari sa kalsada. Tawagan mo lang ako — tulungan kita.
```

### If they are on a bridge, a shoulder with fast traffic, or the EGSA expressway

```
{{NAME}}, naririnig kita. Nasa **mabigat na traffic** ka ba?

Kung **oo** — **HUWAG** lumabas ng sasakyan. **Seatbelt on, hazards on**, manatili sa loob. Ako ang naka-drive papunta sa iyo at makikita kita. Hihintayin kita.

Kung **hindi** — lumabas ka nang maingat at pumunta sa safe spot, tapos tawagan mo ulit ako para sa exact location mo.
```

### If they insist on driving anyway

```
Sige, naiintindihan ko. Pero **pakiusap** — dahan-dahan lang at bawat kada ayon.

Pagsasabi ko nito dahil gusto namin **tayo at pamilya mo** makarating nang buo. Kung may naglabag, hindi po namin maipapaliit yung bagay.

Kung daan ka na, i-message ka lang kung nasa ka na. Tanggapin ka namin kahit anong oras.
```

> Do not argue further. Record the conversation, note the time, and tell the
> owner. This is the one place where being overridden is a serious problem.

## 7. 🚫 NEVER-PROMISE LIST

> **Print this. Tape it next to the screen. Non-negotiable.**

### 7.1 🚫 Never give a price over Messenger

**Not even a rough one. Not even a range you are "pretty sure" about.**

**Say instead:**
> "Ang exact na presyo, after ng inspection lang. Pero para sa idea, mga ganito "
> "yung range naming — subject to inspection."

**Why:** you will get the model, the variant and the condition wrong. Then the
customer arrives expecting a number, you give a different one, and they believe
you lied. The trust is gone and it does not come back.

**This is the #1 anti-overcharge rule in the whole business.** Following it is
the cheapest marketing the shop has.

### 7.2 🚫 Never guarantee an outcome

| Don't say | Say |
| --- | --- |
| "Ayusin namin yan" | "Titingnan namin yan" |
| "Guaranteed maaayos" | "Itatama namin, at ipapakita namin sa iyo" |
| "Mawawala na yun na vibration" | "Maraming dahilan kaysa vibration. Pakikita mo sa amin." |
| "Hindi na yan mag-aago" | "Iyon ang sinasabi mo, at naririnig namin. Pakisama mo kami." |

**We can promise what we do. We cannot promise what a car does.** Say what you
will check and what you will show them.

### 7.3 🚫 Never claim credentials we cannot prove

Banned unless the owner has shown the document, **today**:

- "Certified" · "Licensed" · "Accredited"
- "Authorized dealer" · "Official branch" · "Factory trained"
- "Warranty" — unless there is a real written warranty
- "B accredited by [anything]"

The only safe version:
> "Local shop kami dito sa Tuyo. Lahat ng ginagawa namin, sinusuportahan namin. "
> "May workmanship guarantee ka sa resibo mo."

### 7.4 🚫 Never invent urgency

Banned:

- ~~"Last 2 slots!"~~ unless there are genuinely two
- ~~"Ending tonight!"~~ unless it is genuinely tonight
- ~~"Limited time only!"~~
- ~~"Slam dunk, this week only!"~~
- ~~"Reserved na yan!"~~

**Say instead:**
> "Tayo ito hanggang [real date]."

Real dates are stronger. Fake scarcity, once spotted, ends the relationship.

### 7.5 🚫 Never blame the last technician or the last shop

**Always.** Even when the previous shop genuinely messed up.

| Don't say | Say |
| --- | --- |
| "Ang gulo ng pinakababa" | "Narinig ko na. Tara, titingnan ko ng maayos." |
| "Sira na yan dati" | "Kung ganyan na, makakatulong kami sabihin kung kaya pa bang ayusin o kailangan na palitan." |
| "Hindi yan tamang gawa" | "Hindi ko maipupuna yun kasi hindi ko siya nakita. Pero narito na kami." |

**Why:** it looks petty, and it hands the previous shop a free excuse. "Ang
gulo kayo" — the customer's instinct is to defend whoever just criticized a
colleague.

### 7.6 🚫 Never ask for a review in exchange for anything

> "Salamat sa pagbisita! Kung maganda, tulong sa isang Google review?"

That is fine. This is not:

> "Salamat! Lagyan mo ng 5 stars, may libre kang pag-aalin."

**Asking for a specific star rating violates Google policy and can get the
profile removed — which costs the shop its entire local search position.**
Always ask for an *honest* review.

### 7.7 🚫 Never post a customer's name, plate or photo without permission

Not in a comment reply. Not in a story. Not "just a first name".

> "Hi Maria! Ito na yung bagong brakes ng Fortuner mo 😊"

That needs permission. Ask: "Ok lang ba kung i-post ko yung car mo? Walang plate,
walang pangalan. Gusto mo ba?"

### 7.8 🚫 Never say "we'll be there in 10 minutes" and then be 40 minutes

Roadside timing is a promise. Give the ETA you believe, then **call before you
arrive** if you are running late. A late call is forgivable. A silent 30 minutes
on the Fourlanes is not.

### 7.9 🚫 Never make a customer feel stupid

> "Dapat ninyo alam yan" · "Bakit ginawa ninyo yan?" · "Simple lang yan"

If they do not know something about cars, that is normal. That is why the shop
exists.

### 7.10 🚫 Never keep them waiting without an update

If you cannot reply in 15 minutes, send:
> "Pasensya na, busy kami ngayon. Babalikan namin ka sa loob ng [real time]."

A silence reads as being ignored, which is worse than a slow reply.

## 8. Booking, rescheduling and complaints

### EYG-08b — "I-book ko na ba?"

```
Ayos, i-book ka na.

I-send mo lang:
1. **Plate number**
2. **Make/model ng sasakyan**
3. **Anong kailangan** (PMS? tire? brake?)
4. **Kung kailangan namin ngayon o pwede bukas**

I-ha-book namin at magse-send ng confirmation. Kung may tanong, tawag mo rin lang kami.
```

### EYG-19b — "Puwede ko ba i-reschedule?"

```
Oo, kaya namin palitan.

I-send mo lang yung **reference number** at **bagong petsa o oras**, at ilipat namin. Walang problema.
```

### EYG-20 — "We'll confirm shortly"

> The macro for when a mechanic needs to actually look at the car.

```
Sige, tinitingnan ko ngayon. **Babalikan namin ka sa loob ng [realistic time]** — usually within the hour, kapag hindi kami busy sa ibang customer.

Kung may tanong ka habang naghihintay, message mo lang dito.
```

> **Rule: only send this if you can actually hold to the time.** "Shortly"
> without a number is the single most common way a small business loses a
> customer who was ready to buy.

### EYG-21 — Complaint: something went wrong

```
{{NAME}}, sorry talaga. Naiintindihan ko kung bakit ka nanghihindi — at tama ka.

Ito ang gagawin ko:
1. I-chec-check ko yung case mo.
2. **Tatawagan ka ako mismo** para marinig namin ang side mo.
3. Sasabihin namin sa **exact** kung ano ang nangyari at kung ano ang gagawin namin.

Hindi mo kailangang magbayad ulit. Ang hinahanap lang namin ay matuto at ayusin yung problema.
```

### EYG-22 — Apology, short and clean

```
Sorry, {{NAME}}. Tama ka, napaka-sorry kami.

Aayusin namin ngayon. Kikipag-ugnayan kami sa iyo sa loob ng [real time] para sa next step.

Hindi namin sasabihing "normal lang yan" — hindi, hindi normal. Isa lang ang tama dito, at yan ang gagawin namin.
```

> **Never use "normal lang yan" or "ganon talaga" to dismiss a complaint.** It
> is the fastest way to make an angry customer angrier.

### EYG-23 — Overbooking: the bay is full but they drove in

```
{{NAME}}, sorry — **puno ang bay ngayon** at ayoko kayo na maghintay ng di-tiyak na oras.

Dalawa ang pwede:
1. **Reschedule** ako ngayon para sa bukas (or kahit anong oras na kaya mo).
2. **Tawag ako** kung emergency — kapag may kailangan kang ayusin agad, tutulong kami.

Alin sa dalawa, kaya mo ba?
```

### EYG-24 — Towing coordination

```
{{NAME}}, naiintindihan ko. Hindi ka safe na mag-drive doon — tama ka.

Ito ang gagawin namin:
1. **Huwag mo muna i-start** o i-drive.
2. **Nasa safe spot ka** — hazards on, seatbelt on.
3. I-send mo: **exact location** at **plate number**.

Titingkan namin kung may partner na makakabayo o kung kailangan ng tow. **Tatawagan ka namin bago mag-set off** — hindi ka maghihintay nang walang alam.
```

## 10. Response time targets

| Channel | First reply | Resolution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Messenger (shop hours) | **under 5 min** | Same day |
| Messenger (closed) | Auto-reply instantly, human next morning | Next morning |
| Phone during shop hours | Ring, answer | — |
| Phone outside shop hours | Voicemail with shop hours + roadside number | — |
| WhatsApp | under 10 min | Same day |

⚠️ **A 5-minute first reply beats a perfect answer 3 hours later**, every time.
Send "Tinitingnan ko, babalikan ka" and then actually answer. Reply time is the
single biggest measurable driver of Messenger-to-booking conversion for a shop
this size.

## 11. Handover checklist for whoever is on the phone

```
[ ] First reply under 5 minutes

[ ] NO price given
