# META ADS — EYG Tire & Auto Care

> **Owner:** marketing agent · **Budget:** ₱20/day test
> **Verdict up front:** paid ads are **Q3 or later**. Word of mouth and the
> Google Business Profile will out-spend this by a factor of five for the first
> six months. This document is written so that when the time comes, ₱20/day is
> spent correctly rather than lost.

---

## 1. Why this is Q3 and not Q1

**The economics of a Balanga tyre shop, honestly:**

| Channel | Cost to acquire one customer | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Word of mouth | **₱0** | The main engine. Referrals do not stop. |
| Google Business Profile (organic) | **₱0** | 2 reviews/week. See `GEO-LOCAL-SEO.md`. |
| Facebook organic | **₱0** | The bank in `social.ts`. |
| Messenger (inbound) | **₱0** | Free if someone answers in 5 minutes. |
| Meta Ads | **₱40–120** | A tracked referral, and an ad that also builds the brand. |

₱20/day = ₱600/month. That buys roughly 5–15 clicks at a ₱40–120 CPC for a
local tyre shop, or 1–2 calls. **If the shop cannot serve those calls, the money
is better spent on a discount for the customers it already has.**

**Start Meta Ads when all three of these are true:**
1. GBP is complete, has 20+ reviews, and ranking for "tire shop Balanga".
2. The bay is 60%+ occupied on a normal day (you have capacity to sell).
3. There are real photos of the real bay.

**If all three are not true, spend the ₱20 on this instead:** asking five past
customers by name for a Google review. That is worth more than ₱600 of Meta
spend.

## 2. Targeting — the actual geography

### 2.1 Location targeting for a Balanga tyre shop

**Use the *radius around the shop*, not city names.** City-targeting wastes
money on Balanga City outskirts with no customers.

| Ring | Radius from EGSA Fourlanes | Towns covered | Priority |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | 0–10 km | Tuyo, Balanga City proper, Pilar, Bataan | **⭐ Primary** |
| **Local** | 10–25 km | Hermosa, Orani, Mariveles (partial), San Marcelino, Pilar, Balanga barangays | **⭐ Primary** |
| **Commuter** | 25–60 km | Subic, Olongapo, Dinalupihan, Iba, Bocaue, Bulakan | Secondary |
| **Extended** | 60–120 km | Baguio, Pampanga, Tarlac, Nueva Ecija | **Only for tyres** |

⚠️ **SUGGESTED — REQUIRES OWNER CONFIRMATION.** Distances from EGSA Fourlanes
are planning estimates. The owner must confirm the shop's actual service area
and how far it will realistically drive for a roadside call-out before these
radii are used. **Over-reaching on radius is the fastest way to pay for clicks
from people 40 km away who will never come.**

**Geo settings — non-negotiable:**
- ✅ Location **Presence**: "People living in or regularly in your targeted
  locations"
- ❌ **NOT** "Everyone in your targeted locations" — this reaches people on
  holiday who will never drive to Tuyo
- ✅ Exclude areas the shop genuinely cannot serve

### 2.2 Audience — three initial audiences

**Audience A — COMMUTER CORRIDOR** (the best value in a Balanga market)
- Ages 28–55
- Balanga, Bataan + 40 km
- Interest in: Toyota, Mitsubishi, Honda, Nissan (popular local brands),
  automotive maintenance
- Behaviour: engaged shoppers, "online shopping" buyers

**Audience B — NEW / FIRST CAR OWNER** (highest LTV)
- Ages 20–30
- Balanga, Bataan + 40 km
- Interests: driving school, cars, automotive, driver's licence
- Why: first-time car owners are the least price-anchored and the most loyal
  once trusted

**Audience C — FAMILY CAR** (best for PMS, alignment, tyres)
- Ages 32–55
- Balanga / Bataan + 40 km
- Interests: family, SUV, Toyota Fortuner, Mitsubishi Xpander, Honda CR-V
- Why: bigger vehicles, bigger tyres, bigger jobs

## 3. Ad set structure — 3 ad sets (mandated)

> **At ₱20/day, run exactly 3 ad sets. Not more.**
> More ad sets = less data per ad set = worse delivery = wasted budget. With
> ₱20/day and typical CPC of ₱40–120, expect only 0–1 clicks per hour.

### AD SET 1 — 🚨 EMERGENCY ROADSIDE *(highest intent, tightest targeting)*

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Leads → Click to Call |
| **Budget** | ₱8/day (40%) |
| **Radius** | 0–15 km from EGSA Fourlanes |
| **Location presence** | "Living in or regularly in" |
| **Age** | 25–60 |
| **Gender** | All |
| **Languages** | English, Tagalog, Taglish |
| **Audience** | Broad within the radius. **No interest stacking.** Emergency intent is triggered by the moment, not by interests. |
| **Placement** | Advantage+ placements (auto), **but exclude Audience Network and Reels** — a tyre ad in the Audience Network shows on random apps |

**Creative:** a single image — the shop frontage at night, lights on, with the
word **"BUKAS 24/7"** ⚠️ *(only if genuinely true — see §6)* or **"ROADSIDE
HELP"** with the phone number large.

**Copy:**
```
Kapa na sa Fourlanes?

Tire na-flat, kulang na sa presyon, o nag-i-warning ang car mo? Tawagan mo
lang kami. Sabihin mo lang kung saan ka.

📍 EGSA Fourlanes, Tuyo, Balanga

[Number]
```

**Why this ad set gets 40%:** roadside is the highest-margin, highest-urgency,
most-price-insensitive call. One job pays for a year of ads.

| **Placements** | Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Marketplace (⚠️ Marketplace may restrict services) |

**Creative:** a car on the lift mid-PMS. **A real photo, not stock.**

**Copy variant A (transparency-led):**

Magkano ba talaga ang PMS?

### AD SET 3 — 🛞 TYRES *(the cheapest leads, the longest cycle)*

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| **Objective** | Leads → Messaging (cheapest, fastest lead type) |
| **Budget** | ₱5/day (25%) |
| **Radius** | 0–40 km (wider — tyre buyers travel further) |
| **Location presence** | "Living in or regularly in" |
| **Age** | 25–60 |
| **Audiences** | Commuter corridor + automotive interest |
| **Note** | This is the ad set most likely to be paused. Tyre buying is seasonal and slow. |

**Creative:** the tyre rack, straight on. **A photo of tyres is the most
honest ad in this whole document** — it says "we have these" and it is literally
verifiable.

**Copy:**
```
Tire na dapat i-check?

Hindi mo na kailangang guess yung size. Basahin sa sidewall — nandyan
lang, usually 205/55 R16 ganun.

I-send sa amin yung size (o make/model/year ng sasakyan mo) at sasabihin
namin kung ano ang kaya namin gawin at magkano.

No stock photo, no invented stock. Sasabihin namin kung wala kami.
```

## 4. Creative specs

### Image specs

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Resolution** | 1080 × 1080 (1:1) or 1080 × 1350 (4:5). 4:5 uses more feed space. |
| **File size** | Under 1 MB. Facebook compresses hard — upload sharp. |
| **Format** | JPG. Not PNG (too heavy). Not HEIC (will not process). |
| **Text on image** | **Minimal.** 5 words maximum. Facebook reduces text-heavy images. |
| **Contrast** | Yellow-on-black (`#FCC605` / `#06060A`) — the EYG brand. Test first. |
| **Face count** | 1–2 actual people with consent. Faces outperform. |
| **Logo** | Bottom-left, small, not centred. |

### Reel specs (₱0 production cost — most brands here should test these)

| Element | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| **Length** | 8–15 seconds. Shorter is better for cold audiences. |
| **Aspect** | 9:16 (1080 × 1920) |
| **Frame rate** | 30 fps |
| **Captions** | **Always.** 85% watch muted. Text on screen, every word. |
| **Hook** | First 3 seconds. Face or movement or a striking visual. |
| **Music** | Use Meta's royalty-free library only. |
| **Filename** | `eyg-reel-01.mp4` — not `viral_final_FINAL2.mp4` |

### Format performance expectations

| Format | Best for | Clicks | Leads | Cost per lead |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Static image, frontage | Roadside | High | High | Best |
| Carousel (4–5 cards) | Education, PMS | Medium | High | Best for cheap leads |
| Reel, real bay footage | Brand, trust | High | Medium | Mid |
| Video, talking head | Trust | Low | High | Mid |

### Static image copy on the graphic

> **Text ON the image should be the hook, not the details.** Facebook will show
> this to someone scrolling. They will read 3 words, not 30.

**Roadside:** `ROADSIDE NA. TAYO AGAD.`
**PMS:** `ANG PRESYO? SASABIHIN NAMIN BAGO MAG-START.`
**Tyres:** `TIRE NA KAYA? I-SEND ANG SIZE MO.`

## 5. Copy variants

A/B test one variable at a time. Never change headline **and** image **and**
audience simultaneously — you will learn nothing.

### Roadside — 3 variants

```
A: Kapa na sa Fourlanes? Tawagan mo lang kami.
B: Flat tire sa kalsada? Nandiyan lang kami.
C: Warning light? Sabihin mo kung ano — sasabihin namin kung puwede pa bang mag-drive.
```

Variant **C** is the trust play. It may not win on CTR and it will win on lead
quality, because it filters out tyre-shopping browsers and attracts the people
who actually need help.

### PMS — 3 variants

```
A: PMS. Isang bay visit. Eksaktong presyo bago mag-start.
B: Ang PMS, ang bayad, ang lahat — walang nating ipinupunta na hindi mo alam.
C: Madaling i-book online. Papunta ka sa Fourlanes, tapos ka na sa pag-aalala.
```

### Tyres — 3 variants

```
A: Basahin mo yung size sa sidewall. I-send lang sa amin.
B: Tayo ng tyre. Kumusta ang stock? Tanungin mo lang.
C: Kaysa maghula ng size, basa mo lang yung nakalagay sa iyong tire.
```

### The "subject to inspection" footer — mandatory

> **Every single ad with a price must carry this.** Not in the image. In the
> copy or the headline. Legal protection and, more usefully, the trust
> differentiator.
>
> "Final price confirmed after inspection. No surprise on the invoice."

## 6. Compliance — read this before you publish anything

> **Meta's advertising policies and Philippine consumer law are both enforced.
> A rejected ad is fine. A banned ad account is not fine.**

### 6.1 🚫 No misleading pricing

**Banned:** showing a price that is not what the customer will pay, using a
"from ₱X" that never applies, showing a strikethrough that never existed.

**Required:** any price in an ad must be a real price at the real counter, or
must be accompanied by "final price confirmed after inspection."

⚠️ **Until the owner confirms every price, run ads with NO prices in them.**
The transparency angle ("we confirm before we start") outperforms a price in
this market anyway, and it is impossible to get wrong.

### 6.2 🚫 No personal-attribute (sensitive-category) targeting

**Meta prohibits implying you know the user's personal attributes.**

| Banned | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "Are you a Honda owner? Your brakes are due." | Implying knowledge of the user's identity |
| "Your car is due for a service." | Same — "your" is the problem |
| "Boys and their cars 🚗" | Gender targeting adjacent to a product |
| "For mothers on the road" | Gender + life-stage targeting |
| "Filipino drivers like you" | Ethnicity targeting |

**Safe alternatives:**

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Your car is due" | "Most cars need this every 10,000 km" |
| "Honda owners" | "Common Honda brake job — we measure before replacing" |
| "For moms" | "For the school run and the weekend" |
| "Are you a Toyota owner?" | *(Ask a question, don't assert)* "What year is your Toyota?" |

**Targeting settings that must stay off:**
- ❌ Detailed targeting → *Everyone* not *Custom Audience*
- ❌ Special Ad Categories — **not required** here, do not set
- ✅ Audience → Advantage+ audience (broad, no interest detail)

### 6.3 🚫 No before/after that implies a result

**Banned:** "Before" and "After" images that suggest the shop produced the
outcome, with no context.

The legal test: could a reasonable person read this ad as a guarantee that the
*same* result will happen for them? If yes, it is banned.

| Banned | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Before/after brake pads with no explanation | "Brake pad at 2 mm — measured before removing. This car's pads were at the limit, so we replaced them." |
| "Worn tire → new tire" implying extended life | "A tyre that arrived below the wear bar, and the replacement we fitted." |
| Dirty bay → clean bay implying a service result | **Never use bay cleaning as a "before/after".** It is misleading. |

**Required context on every work photo:** the vehicle, the measurement, and what
was actually done. **Never caption a set-up as an outcome.**

### 6.4 🚫 No personal health or financial claims

| Banned | Why |
| --- | --- |
| "Stay safe on the road" implying a safety guarantee | Outcome guarantee |
| "Prevent accidents" | Outcome guarantee + health-adjacent claim |
| "Affordable financing available" | Requires proof of the actual terms |
| "0% interest" | Requires proof, and heavy restrictions in PH |

**Safe:** "We check your brakes and tell you the measurement."

### 6.5 🚫 No bait-and-switch on urgency

**Banned:** "Today only!", "Last chance!", "Only 3 slots left", countdowns that
reset, "Act now" on a permanent offer.

If an offer genuinely ends, say the real date. **Always.** ("Valid until
30 November 2026" is safe. "Valid until tonight" when it is not, is banned and
is a lie.)

### 6.6 🚫 No personal attributes in the creative

Never in the image text either:

| Banned in image copy | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| "Your 2015 Honda needs new brakes" | "Brake pads at 2 mm — measured before replacement" |
| "Are your tires bald?" | "Can you see the grooves? If not, you're past due." |
| "Moms on the road" | "For the school run" |

### 6.7 🚫 No claims about a vehicle or its safety

**Never say:** "This will fix your vibration" · "Your car is safe" · "We will
make it last longer" · "Guaranteed to stop the pulling."

**Always say:** what we will do (measure, inspect, quote) and what the customer
will see (the measurement, the old part, the itemised invoice).

### 6.8 🚫 Business claims that need proof

Only claim credentials with a document on hand:

| Do not say | Unless |
| --- | --- |
| "Licensed" · "Certified" · "Accredited" | You have the certificate and can show it |
| "Authorized dealer" | You are an authorised dealer, in writing |
| "Official service center" | Same |
| "B accredited by [org]" | You are |
| "4.9 stars from 500+ customers" | You are, and it is real |

⚠️ `site.ts` currently has `ratingValue: 4.9, ratingCount: 0`. **Do not put a
star rating in any ad.** Fabricated social proof is an outright policy violation
and the highest-risk item in this document.

### 6.9 Special Ad Category for credit / instalment

If an ad mentions **credit instalment**, Meta may classify it as a
**Credit** special ad category, which has its own very restrictive targeting
requirements. **Rule: do not run ads mentioning instalment until you have
confirmed with the owner and checked the current policy.** Use "Ask us about
payment options" instead.

## 7. The ₱20/day test — 14-day decision tree

### 7.1 Setup

**Phase 0 (day −3 to 0):** create the ad account structure without spending.

- Campaign: `EYG Local — Test — {{YYYY-MM}}`
- **Objective:** Leads
- **Buying type:** Lowest cost (no bid cap at this budget)
- **Budget:** Advantage campaign budget, ₱20/day — **do NOT split the budget at
  campaign level**, or you starve one ad set. Set campaign budget ₱20/day with
  Advantage budget distribution; ad sets have no individual budget.

> **Why Advantage budget:** with ₱20/day, manual splits fight each other. One
> ad set will go hungry all day and you will conclude it does not work, when the
> real problem is that it got no impressions.

- 3 ad sets as per §3
- 2 ad variations per ad set (one hook, one creative)
- **Attribution:** 7-day click, 1-day view (standard for a local lead gen
  business)
- **Pixel:** ⚠️ Only if the owner accepts it. For a shop this small, tracking may
  not be worth the privacy and complexity. **Skip the pixel. Optimise for
  leads.**

### 7.2 The 14-day protocol

| Day | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Launch. Set a daily reminder at 9 AM to check once, not constantly. |
| 1–3 | **Touch nothing.** Not one edit. Early edits reset the learning phase. |
| 4 | Check delivery. Is every ad set getting at least some impressions? |
| 7 | First real read. Check §7.3 metrics. |
| 10 | **Only edit if an ad has spent ₱70+ with zero leads.** |
| 14 | Decision. Go to §7.4. |

> **The most common Meta ad mistake at this budget is editing daily.** Every
> edit resets learning. At ₱20/day you get maybe 15–25 clicks in 14 days. That
> is not a lot of data — but it is enough to spot a clear winner.

### 7.3 The 7-day read

**Two numbers matter. Everything else is noise.**

| Metric | Where | Good | Concern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Cost per lead** | Ads Manager | Under ₱150 for roadside | Over ₱300 |
| **Lead-to-booked** | Your own count | 1 in 3 or better | Under 1 in 5 |

**Also check:**

| Signal | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Reach concentrated in 10–15 km | ✅ Correct |
| Reach concentrated 40 km+ | ❌ Radius too wide. Tighten. |
| Only one ad set getting delivery | ⚠️ Advantage starving one. Manually boost it once. |
| Ad sets all dead | ⚠️ Radius too tight or audience too small. |
| High reach, low CTR (<1%) | ❌ Creative problem. Change the hook. |
| High CTR (>3%), low leads | ❌ Landing problem. Fix the page or the phone handling. |

**The tracking link.** Ask every phone caller one question:

> "Nakita mo yung ad namin sa Facebook?"

Count it in a notebook for 14 days. ⚠️ **This manual count is more reliable than
Meta's own attribution at ₱20/day.** At this budget, Meta's data is too thin to
trust.

### 7.5 Special case — a lead that needs explanation

Sometimes the right read is "the ad is fine, the customer is not ours yet."

**Ask the customer on the phone:** "What made you message instead of just
calling?"

If the answer is "I saw it and thought it was cheap" but they are looking for
the cheapest possible option — they are not the customer. A tyre shop cannot win
that customer, and paying to reach them is waste.

**This matters more than the CPC.** One tyre customer who comes back every 6
months for 5 years is worth far more than 100 price-shoppers.

## 8. Seasonal calendar for ads

Ads should follow the seasons, not run year-round.

| Month | Lead campaign | Ad to run |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **January** | Year-end post-holiday inspection | "Tire pressure and brakes after the holidays" |
| **February** | Payday / 13th month | PMS offer. Budget up. |
| **March** | Holy Week | ⚠️ Check shop closure. Vehicle prep. |
| **April** | Summer travel prep | Roadside + tyres. |
| **May** | Summer holidays | Tyres, tyres, tyres. Best tyre month. |
| **June** | **Rainy season starts** | Rainy Season Safety Bundle. Shift budget to roadside. |
| **July** | Rainy peak | Roadside ad set at 50% of budget. |
| **August** | 9G/10G toll days ⚠️ verify dates | "Do not wait until Undas." Roadside. |
| **September** | **Rainy + Undas building** | Bookings for Undas. Raise budget. |
| **October** | Undas | Roadside + clearance tyres. |
| **November** | Christmas / Undas peak | Biggest budget of the year. |
| **December** | Year-end | Book before the rush. Spend drops 50% after the 24th. |

**Budget reallocation is the highest-leverage lever at ₱20/day.** Moving the
same money from a PMS ad in July to a roadside ad in September beats increasing
the budget.

## 10. Do not do these things

- ❌ **Boost a post.** Boosting prioritises engagement, not leads. Use the Ads
  Manager with a Leads objective.
- ❌ **Run ads with a price that has not been confirmed by the owner.**
- ❌ **Use a stock garage photo.** A local will recognise it instantly, and it
  is misleading.
- ❌ **Target interests AND location AND age AND behaviour all at once.** At
  ₱20/day you will get no impressions at all.
- ❌ **Run ads during shop hours only.** Commuters and stranded drivers search
  at night. Turn on delivery when your phone is reachable, and let Meta find
  the rest.
- ❌ **Edit the ad more than once every 7 days.**
- ❌ **Buy followers or likes.** They do not book cars.
- ❌ **Use "Boost Post" on a customer photo.** It is a trust asset, not an ad.
- ❌ **Turn ads off because a week was slow.** Seasonal, not data.
