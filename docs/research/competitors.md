## 5. Five "why EYG wins" arguments — each one defensible from evidence

> Every argument below is tied to a **source in this repository**. None relies on a
> superlative the shop cannot prove. If any one of them stops being true on launch day,
> delete the corresponding claim from the site.

### 5.1 "You will know the price before you leave the house."

- **Evidence:** of five local competitor listings read in full, **zero publish a price.**
  EYG has no published prices either — but it *will*.
- **The claim:** publish an honest PHP range for every service on `/services`, with a
  standing note that the final figure is confirmed on the job order.
- **Why it wins:** the single largest source of anxiety in a Philippine auto shop is
  *"magkano ba?"* — the fear of an unknown bill. Publishing a range converts that fear
  into a booking. It is also **legally safer**, not riskier: a stated range plus a
  job-order confirmation is far better evidence of fair dealing under the Consumer Act
  than a bare "inquire at the counter".
- **Guard-rail:** ranges must be marked `SUGGESTED — REQUIRES OWNER CONFIRMATION` until
  signed off, and must never be presented as a quote.

### 5.2 "Book a bay before you drive over. We'll have the bay ready."

- **Evidence:** **no local competitor has any online booking** — verified across the
  official city directory.
- **The claim:** `/book` with real slot availability and an SMS confirmation.
- **Why it wins:** combined with the verified hours (Mon–Sat 08:00–17:00), the site can
  tell a commuter at 07:30 *"we open at 8, you're booked for 8:30."* That is a
  genuinely different interaction from phoning a shop that may not answer.
- **This is the argument that most rewards good engineering** — the booking system is
  only a differentiator if the availability data is real and the confirmation actually
  sends. A booking form that silently fails is worse than no form at all.

### 5.3 "Tyre brands we are actually listed to sell — and we'll tell you which ones."

- **Evidence:** EYG TIRE TRADING is a **verified "4W tire dealer"** in both the
  **Michelin** and **BFGoodrich** Philippines dealer locators
  (`michelin.com.ph/auto/dealer-locator/batangas/yfvejbu-eyg-tire-trading`,
  `bfgoodrich.com.ph/auto/dealer-locator/batangas/yfvejbu-eyg-tire-trading`).
  Independently confirmed in stock: Radar Renegade R/T+ 265/65R17, Blacklion Mix
  11R22.5, Deestone 185/65-14, PETRONAS engine oil, Amaron Jade AGM batteries.
- **The claim:** *"A listed Michelin and BFGoodrich dealer in Bataan."* — that is
  **checkable by anyone** and it is the single most credible sentence EYG can write.
- **Why it wins:** brand-authorised claims are rare locally and instantly separating.
  Local independents can only say "we have tyres."
- 🚨 **Guard-rail — read `facebook-intel.md` §2.7 first.** Print **only** the brands
  that are evidenced: **Michelin, BFGoodrich, PETRONAS, Amaron, Radar, Blacklion,
  Deestone.** Do **not** print Bridgestone, Goodyear, Dunlop, Maxxis or Yokohama —
  there is no evidence for any of them, and a false brand badge is both a legal problem
  and the single fastest way to lose a customer who knows tyres. Do **not** print
  "official Amaron distributor" without written confirmation from Amaron Philippines.

### 5.4 "We do tyres *and* the service that keeps them alive."

- **Evidence:** the verified 10-service list spans the whole ownership cycle — PMS, change
  oil, brakes, underchassis, alignment *and* camber correction, balancing, mounting and
  repair, nitrogen, battery, OBD. Real completed jobs are documented too: steering rack
  replacement, Amaron AGM battery installation, PETRONAS oil service.
- **The claim:** *"Tyres and the workshop that maintains them, under one roof."*
- **Why it wins:** tyre-only shops lose you at the first alignment and alignment-only
  shops lose you at the first puncture. EYG holds both, and the evidence is its own
  documented job history, not a claim.
- **Guard-rail:** this argument covers exactly the ten verified services plus the
  individually evidenced jobs. It does **not** cover undercoating-as-a-product, AC
  repair, engine tune-up, shocks, CVL, glass, roadside or motorcycles. Those are in
  `content/services.md` flagged *needs owner confirmation* and must not be used in
  positioning until confirmed — even though three of the local competitors
  (Allen, Justine Angelos, Vernick) already advertise AC work, which means **if EYG
  claims AC service it will be held to a higher standard than a silent competitor.**

### 5.5 "Every number we publish, we can stand behind."

- **Evidence:** the shop's own Facebook voice is **concrete and specific** — it names
  exact tyre sizes and compounds ("265/65R17 RADAR RENEGADE R/T+"), names exact parts
  ("Installed DIN80 Amaron Jade AGM battery", "Replaced Steering Rack Assembly"), and
  states hours plainly. It makes **no** claims of "certified", "licensed", "guaranteed"
  or "genuine parts only".
- **The claim:** publish the job order before work starts; publish what is *not*
  included; publish a written workmanship guarantee **only once the owner has agreed to
  one in writing**.
- **Why it wins:** in a market where nobody publishes anything, restraint reads as
  competence. It is also the only one of these five arguments that gets *stronger* as
  the review count grows — whereas price claims get weaker the moment a competitor
  undercuts.
- 🚨 **Guard-rail:** `trust.workmanshipGuaranteeDays: 30` in `src/config/site.ts` is
  **fabricated and must not ship.** A written warranty is a legal commitment. Publish
  one only when the owner signs it.

**Two arguments EYG must NOT make.** "The only tyre shop on EGSA Fourlanes" is false —
Jed-M is at 4 Lanes Egsa. "Balanga's most trusted" is unfalsifiable. Both are also the
kind of claim that invites exactly the kind of public, screenshot-able review response
that a 308-follower shop cannot afford.

## 6. Claims competitors make that EYG must NOT copy

Read this as a hard prohibitions list for the copywriter and the marketing agent.

| # | The claim | Why EYG must not copy it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"We use genuine / original parts only"** | Unfalsifiable by the customer and by you. Amaron, PETRONAS, Radar, Blacklion and Deestone are real products you sell, but you cannot prove the negative about every part that ever enters the bay. It is the classic bait for a bait-and-switch accusation. Say the **part brand and model you fitted** instead — which is verifiable and is what the shop already does. |
| 2 | **"Certified / licensed / government-accredited technicians"** | There is **no general Philippine mechanic-licensing scheme** for ordinary automotive service technicians (see `legal-compliance-ph.md` §6). Printing "certificadong mekaniko" invites a customer to ask *certified by whom?* and you will have no answer. If EYG ever earns a genuine technician certification or a brand's training accreditation, that is a real and powerful claim — but it must name the certifying body. |
| 3 | **"We are an authorised dealer"** (for any brand) | EYG's verified brand status is a **Michelin and BFGoodrich *dealer locator listing*** — that is a dealer record, **not** proof of franchise authorisation, exclusive territory, or OEM warranty. Do not upgrade "listed dealer" into "authorised dealer". Michelin and BFGoodrich own the wording on their own site. |
| 4 | **"Official distributor"** for any brand | This is EYG's own unverified self-description for Amaron, appearing only on the older Facebook page. It is a **supply-chain claim about a contractual relationship** you cannot evidence. Get Amaron's written confirmation or drop the word "official". |
| 5 | **"Lowest price in Bataan" / "Sulit" / "Cheapest"** | Provably false the moment a competitor undercuts, and it invites a price war with Motorlandia and 137 independents. Unwinnable, and it converts your differentiator (transparency) back into a commodity race. |
| 6 | **"Unlimited / free lifetime…" anything** | "Free lifetime tyre pressure checks", "lifetime warranty", "unlimited kilometres". Unsustainable as a service-cost liability, and a Consumer Act problem the moment a customer relies on it. |
| 7 | **A countdown timer, "slots left: 3", or "closing soon"** on a shop with three bays | Straight fake urgency. Bounded capacity is real — but **never fabricate scarcity**. See `risks-dos-donts.md` §3.1. The shop already has 308 followers; a fake-urgency story that leaks is terminal. |
| 8 | **Reusing "First 20 customers get FREEBIES"** | EYG's own opening promo, posted **2025-12-09**, is **10 months expired**. Recycling it would be a false "current offer" under the Consumer Act and a self-inflicted Facebook-comment disaster in a 308-follower community where everyone knows each other. |
| 9 | **Stock-photo "our team" and "our bay"** | The brief bans it. Beyond that, in a town this size, **the actual customers know what EYG's bay and staff look like.** A stock photo is not just a lie, it is a laughable one. Use the real job photos already extracted in `facebook-intel.md` §2.11.3. |
| 10 | **Star ratings, review counts, or "rated 5.0 by Balanga"** | `trust.ratingValue: 4.9` with `ratingCount: 0` in `site.ts` is fabricated and would emit invalid `AggregateRating` structured data. With **no verified Google Business Profile at all**, there is nothing to display. Claiming reviews you do not have is the most legally exposed item in this document. |
| 11 | **"Years of experience" / "Serving Balanga since 20XX"** | Facebook's own `foundingDate` says the Auto Care page was created **2025-10-19** and it opened **2025-12-09**; the older tyre page dates to **2021-03-18**; the brief says **March 2025**; `site.ts` says **2024**. Four different answers. **Claim no year until the owner settles it.** |
| 12 | **"24/7 roadside assistance"** | Nothing on either Facebook page supports it. The brief builds its primary conversion lane (stranded → call → rescue) on this. **This is the highest-risk unverified claim in the entire project** — see `risks-dos-donts.md` §5 and `facebook-intel.md` §4 Q14. The nearest real towing operator in the city directory is MDE Towing Service; if EYG cannot deliver 24/7, do not advertise it. |
| 13 | **Named competitors, disparagingly** | No "the shop down the road overcharges", no naming Jed-M, CMC or anyone. In Balanga that is a customer-facing insult in a market of ~138 businesses. It also creates a defamation exposure. Win on your own information, never on someone else's reputation. |
| 14 | **"Free estimates" / "No-obligation quotes" as a headline offer** | EHG cannot afford to make diagnostics free at scale, and a free estimate creates an obligation to produce one. Say *"we'll confirm the price in writing before we start"* — a promise you can keep — rather than promising a free artefact. |
| 15 | **Any price without a job order behind it** | A published price that a customer can be charged more than, with no written job order, is the exact shape of a Consumer Act unfair-practice complaint. Prices and job orders are a matched pair — ship both or neither. |

## 7. Field-research protocol for the provinces I could not reach

Search engines were blocked (see `facebook-intel.md` §1). **I did not guess at Bulacan,
Pampanga or Batangas shops. Here is the protocol to complete that research** — it is
~90 minutes of work and it should be done by someone with a phone and Google Maps access.

**Priority order — these are the towns that compete for Batoan's traffic:**

| Tier | Town | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Olongapo / Subic / San Fernando, Zambales** | Northbound EGSA traffic; the nearest substitute for a Balanga driver |
| 1 | **Bocaue, Bulacan** | ~1 h south on the highway; **the single biggest diversion threat** for Balanga tyre buyers |
| 1 | **Angeles / San Fernando / Angeles City, Pampanga** | ~1.5 h north; large market, real chains |
| 2 | **Batangas City / Lipa** | Michelin and BFGoodrich both file EYG under Batangas, so the brand's own geography points here |
| 3 | **Subic Bay Freeport / Olongapo** | Freeport has its own vehicle population with no local shop |

**For each of the 8–10 closest, run this and record it in this file:**

1. **Google Maps**: search `tire shop near me`, `auto care Balanga/Bocaue`, `wheel alignment
   <town>`, `PMS <town>`, `undercoating <town>`. **Record the star rating, review count,
   and the top 3 review texts verbatim** — the reviews are the cheapest competitive
   intelligence available anywhere.
2. **Check for a Google Business Profile.** Note whether it is claimed, whether hours are
   filled in, whether photos exist, and whether the **NAP matches** their own website
   and Facebook. NAP mismatch is the most common and most fixable local-SEO defect.
3. **Website check**: exists? mobile-loads in under 3 s on 3G? has a price? has hours?
   has online booking? is there a persistent tap-to-call? **This is EYG's direct
   comparison table** — fill in §3's right-hand column for each competitor.
4. **Facebook**: page or no page? follower count? last post date? **Are they posting job
   photos?** This is the single most diagnostic question — a shop that posts real job
   photos is actively competing for the same customer, and one that hasn't posted in six
   months is not.
5. **Waze** and **Apple Maps** listing — is it claimed? These are heavily used by
   Filipino drivers and are usually neglected.
6. **Menu (Facebook "Price List" tab)** — the fastest way to find a local price. Many
   small shops publish a price list there and nowhere else. **This is how to replace my
   `SUGGESTED` price ranges with real market data.**

**Then feed the findings into:** `content/services.md` (price calibration),
`docs/funnel/` (which competitor the "vs." copy must beat), and
`content/faqs.md` (the questions their reviews reveal customers are already asking).

## 9. The one-paragraph version

Balanga City has **138 registered automotive businesses** and EYG is competing against a
named neighbour — **Jed-M Tires & Batteries & Service Center, 4 Lanes Egsa, Tuyo** —
that sits on the same street with an almost identical service mix and possibly a decade
more trading history. Scale players (Motorlandia, Motortrade) are in the city but not on
EGSA. **Not one of the five local listings I read in full publishes its opening hours, its
prices, a service list, or any way to book.** That absence is the entire opportunity. EYG
cannot win on scale, on price, or on street presence — and it must not try, because the
street is already occupied. It wins on the one thing 138 businesses are collectively not
doing: **telling Balanga drivers, before they leave home, what the job costs, how long it
takes, when the shop is open, and letting them book a bay — all on a phone that answers.**
Build that and the strategy takes care of itself.
